# Master Planning Prompt

> Copy everything below the line and paste it at the start of a new planning session.
> Fill in the two bracketed fields. Everything else the AI figures out from the URL.

---

You're planning a premium website build. Before writing a single word of the plan, do a thorough analysis of everything below. Then lock in a complete, actionable site plan.

**Business:** [Business Name]  
**Existing site to analyze:** [URL — or "none, work from what I describe below"]

---

## Step 1 — Deep Analysis (do this first, show your findings)

**Brand Extraction**
- Exact colors with hex values from the existing site or logo
- Font personality — formal or casual, serif or sans, modern or classic
- Visual tone — luxury, approachable, technical, bold, warm, clinical, playful, etc.
- Logo style and how to honor/complement it in the new design

**Business & Audience**
- What this business actually does (go beyond their tagline)
- Who their ideal client is and what emotional state they're in when they're ready to hire/buy
- The specific problem the client has and how this business uniquely solves it
- What makes this business genuinely different from competitors

**Competitor Landscape**
- What do the best-designed sites in this industry do well?
- What are the standard design conventions for this space?
- Where is there a real opportunity to stand out and look better than everyone else?

**Content Audit**
- What usable copy and content already exists?
- What needs to be written fresh?
- What photography or imagery does this site need to feel premium?

---

## Step 2 — Complete Site Plan (after analysis, produce this in full)

**Pages** — every page, its purpose, and its primary conversion action

**Home page section order** — exact sequence from top to bottom, with the purpose of each section. Consider these premium sections (use the ones that fit, skip the ones that don't):
- Cinematic full-screen hero with scene cycling or video loop
- Trust/credibility stats with animated counters
- Services or products grid
- Social proof — scrolling testimonial marquee
- Partner or brand logo wall
- Human/about teaser (face behind the business)
- CTA conversion band (strong mid or end-of-page moment)
- Sticky mobile bottom bar (call + primary action)

**Industry-specific sections** — what does THIS industry need that isn't on the standard list above?

**3 Wow Factors** — specific, industry-appropriate design moments that will make this site feel award-worthy. Be specific and visual, not generic. Not "great animations" — describe exactly what, where, and why it works for this business.

**Copy direction** — the tone guide, key messages, headline style, what to emphasize and what to avoid

**Visual direction** — how the brand colors get used (not just "use brand colors"), imagery style, layout density, animation personality, what the site should feel like to scroll through

---

## Step 3 — Lock It In

Make decisions. Do not give me a list of options to choose from — pick the best direction for this specific business and audience, and tell me why it's right. I'll approve, push back, or adjust specific parts.

The output of this session is a locked plan I can hand directly to a developer. Every section, every design decision, every content requirement should be decided before we finish.

When you're ready to lock, summarize the full plan in one clean list so I can review and approve it.
